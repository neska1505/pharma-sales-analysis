QUESTIONS INVESTIGATED
- What are the total sales quantities for each drug category (ATC code)?
- Which three drugs have the highest sales in January 2015, July 2016, September 2017.
- Which drug has sold the most often in 2017?
- Which drug category has the highest average daily sales?
- Are respiratory drugs (R03) sold more during specific months?

SUMMARY
- The pharmaceutical sales analysis was conducted using a dataset containing 2,106 daily records from January 2014 to October 2019, covering eight ATC drug categories.
  
- The N02BE category recorded the highest cumulative sales, with approximately 63,005.40 units, making it the leading category in the dataset. N05B ranked second with 18,645.74 units, followed by R03 with 11,608.82 units. N05C recorded the lowest total sales, at approximately 1,249.96 units.
  
- N02BE ranked first in all three selected periods, demonstrating consistently strong sales performance. N05B ranked second in January 2015 and September 2017, while M01AB occupied third place in July 2016.
January 2015: N02BE (1,044.24), N05B (463.00), and R03 (177.25).
July 2016: N02BE (652.36), N05B (240.00), and M01AB (194.53).
September 2017: N02BE (863.75), N05B (223.00), and R03 (139.00).

- N02BE was the highest-selling category in 2017, recording approximately 9,258.80 units. N05B followed with 2,555.54 units, while R03 recorded 1,893.61 units. This indicates that N02BE maintained its leading position during the year.

- N02BE recorded the highest average daily sales, at approximately 29.92 units per day, followed by N05B at 8.85 units and R03 at 5.51 units. N05C had the lowest average daily sales, at approximately 0.59 units per day.

- The monthly analysis revealed variations in average daily R03 sales across the year. December recorded the highest average, at approximately 7.92 units per day, followed by October at 7.21 and February at 6.90. July recorded the lowest average daily sales, at approximately 2.96 units, followed by August at 3.10.
These findings suggest that respiratory drug sales may exhibit seasonal patterns, with higher average sales in certain months and lower sales during the middle of the year.

- The yearly analysis showed that total sales across the eight categories were highest in 2016, at approximately 25,234.93 units, compared with 20,238.34 units in 2014. Total sales declined to 19,399.37 units in 2017, recovered to 22,884.56 units in 2018, and fell to 17,085.96 units in 2019.

CONCLUSION
- The analysis identified N02BE as the dominant ATC category in cumulative sales, average daily sales, the selected monthly periods, and annual sales for 2017. N05B consistently ranked among the top-selling categories, while R03 displayed noticeable monthly variations, with December recording the highest average daily sales and July the lowest.
